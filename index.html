About PSU Campus Connect

PSU Campus Connect helps students discover events, activities, and opportunities happening across campus.

The goal of the site is to make it easier for students to find ways to connect with the university community and take part in campus life.

  • Discover upcoming campus events in one place.
  • Learn about activities from different student organizations.
  • Find opportunities to meet people, learn, and get involved.
