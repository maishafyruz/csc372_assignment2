Fall Campus Festival

Campus Celebration

October 3, 2026 at 4:00 PM

Location: University Green

The Fall Campus Festival brings students together for an afternoon of music, food, games, and campus activities. Students can explore booths, meet organizations, and enjoy performances throughout the event.

About the Festival

The Fall Campus Festival is one of PSU's largest student events of the semester. It gives students a chance to relax, meet new people, and learn more about campus organizations.

Attendees can expect live music, student organization booths, food, games, and activities throughout the afternoon.

What to Expect

  • Live student performances and music.
  • Food trucks and refreshments.
  • Games and outdoor activities.
  • Information booths from student organizations.

Festival Schedule

  • 4:00 PM: Festival opens and student booths begin.
  • 5:00 PM: Live music performances begin.
  • 6:30 PM: Campus games and activities.
  • 8:00 PM: Closing performance.

Accessibility and Participation

The University Green has accessible walkways and seating areas. Students who need additional accommodations may contact the Office of Student Engagement before the event.

Event Information

Date: October 3, 2026

Time: 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

Location: University Green

Organizer: Office of Student Engagement

Admission: Free for PSU students
